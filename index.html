What this site is

GitHub Pages hosts sites like this one. The files are finished before anyone visits. The browser draws the page. There is no database and no login.

Change a sentence in index.html, save, and refresh. The new words are the new site.

index.html
The page: headings, links, and the project cards.
styles.css
Color, type, and layout, including the phone layout.
script.js
Opens the menu and shows or hides cards when you filter.
